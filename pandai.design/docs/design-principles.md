# Design Principles for the Pandai Design System

## 1. User-Centric Design
Our design system prioritizes the needs and experiences of users. Every component is crafted with usability in mind, ensuring intuitive interactions and accessibility for all users.

## 2. Consistency
Consistency across components and styles is key to creating a cohesive user experience. We adhere to standardized design tokens for colors, typography, and spacing to maintain uniformity throughout the application.

## 3. Flexibility
The design system is built to be flexible, allowing for customization and adaptation to various contexts and branding requirements. Components are designed to be easily configurable while maintaining their core functionality.

## 4. Scalability
Our design system is designed to scale with the needs of the application. Components are modular and reusable, enabling developers to build complex interfaces without redundancy.

## 5. Performance
Performance is a critical consideration in our design system. We optimize components for fast loading times and responsiveness, ensuring a smooth user experience across devices.

## 6. Accessibility
We are committed to making our design system accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. Components are designed to meet accessibility standards, providing features like keyboard navigation and screen reader support.

## 7. Collaboration
The design system fosters collaboration between designers and developers. Clear documentation and guidelines are provided to ensure that all team members can contribute effectively to the design and implementation process.

## 8. Continuous Improvement
We believe in the iterative process of design. Feedback from users and team members is actively sought and incorporated into the design system, allowing for continuous enhancement and refinement of components.
