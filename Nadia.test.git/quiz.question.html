Which of the following correctly converts 180° into radians?

Explanation

To convert degrees to radians, use the formula:

radians = degrees × (π ÷ 180)

Therefore: 180° × (π ÷ 180) = π radians.

Remember that 360° = 2π radians, and 180° = π radians is a fundamental relationship in trigonometry.
